The model is reasonably strong, but the forecase plot reveals on clear weakness:

while the actual temperature continues rising and remains near 25°C (77 °F).

By hours 23–24, it is underpredicting by roughly 3.5–4°C. This is characteristic 
of an overly smooth forecast that handles normal daily cycles better than sustained
warming or a weather-regime transition.
